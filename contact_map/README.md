Here, the ...sum_squares.py scripts were used to calculate the sum of the squared difference contact values found in the SI.
They are written specifically for our datasets, with particular alterations for residue numbers, as capping residues differ between our ArkA12 and ArkA17 constructs, as noted in our paper.
Number of frames is also accounted for after "stride" (frames in simulation trajectory skipped) is determined in the Jupyter Notebooks.

These py scripts are not necessary to generate the contact maps in the Jupyter Notebooks.
